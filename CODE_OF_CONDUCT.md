# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the Streamweld community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience; and
- focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring someone to disclose credentials, production data, prompts, or
  other sensitive material; and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct
and will communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.

Maintainers must respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an
incident. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must not handle that report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces. Project spaces include
the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, Discussions, hosted demos, and
events or communication channels operated on behalf of Streamweld.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior confidentially
through the [private reporting channel](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
Begin the report title with `Code of Conduct` so it can be routed correctly. Do
not place sensitive incident details in a public issue or Discussion.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly. Responses may range from
a private correction or warning to a temporary or permanent restriction from
project spaces, depending on the nature, impact, and pattern of behavior.
Retaliation against someone who reports an incident in good faith is itself a
violation of this Code of Conduct.
